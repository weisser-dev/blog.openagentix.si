*Updated in November 2026 to reflect the current direction. The original article described an earlier agent-platform project; its original version remains in the repository history.*

I build and explore software around AI, coding agents and the interfaces people use to work with them. OpenAgentix is the place where I collect those independent projects and write about what I learn. It is a personal, non-commercial initiative by [weisser-dev](https://github.com/weisser-dev), not a company or a single product platform.

## Different projects, one place to find them

Each project has its own purpose and repository. Some are experiments, some are actively developed, and others are preserved as archives. Their individual repositories are the source of truth for licenses, supported features and current status.

[OpenGlassUI](https://github.com/weisser-dev/openglassui) is an experimental companion that connects coding-agent harnesses to a wearable interface. I am interested in whether a quick glance, a gesture or a short voice response can make it easier to stay involved while an agent works elsewhere. It remains a prototype, and its README documents the limitations.

[Awesome OpenCode](https://github.com/weisser-dev/awesome-opencode) collects agents, skills and configuration for OpenCode. [Open Model Prism](https://github.com/weisser-dev/open-model-prism) is an archived experiment around language-model routing; its source remains available.

The earlier [open-agentix agent-platform repository](https://github.com/open-agentix/open-agentix) also remains available separately. Older articles may describe that project, its original plans or its development practices at a particular point in time. They should not be read as a description of the entire collection or a promise about current features.

## Why write about it?

I want to document useful ideas, working software and the problems that remain. I use AI tools when they help, but I make the decisions about the direction of my projects. A published article is not a guarantee that a feature is complete or that every setup will work the same way.

I prefer to link to the code and identify the limits of an experiment. Anyone interested can inspect a repository, try the tools and decide what is useful. There is no commercial product offering attached to this collection.

Start with the [project overview](https://openagentix.si/) or read my article on [wearables and coding agents](/posts/wearables-and-coding-agents/).
